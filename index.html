How to keep them happy

☀

Light

Most houseplants prefer bright, indirect light. A few feet from a south-facing window is the sweet spot.

💧

Water

Let the top inch of soil dry between waterings. Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering.

🌱

Soil & food

Repot every 1-2 years with fresh, well-draining mix. Feed lightly in spring and summer.
